YouTube Bridge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short:

  • YouTube Bridge is a private tool used by one person (the owner of the YouTube channel @hrushitembe6550) to manage that channel only.
  • It uses YouTube API Services. It reads that channel's own data and uploads that channel's own videos.
  • Data is kept on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

1. What YouTube Bridge is

YouTube Bridge ("the app") is a set of scripts that the channel owner runs on their own computer. It is not a public service: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no other users. The only Google account that signs in to it is the account that owns the channel above.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the app accesses

DataPermissionWhy
Channel name, subscriber/view/video counts, list of uploaded videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and statistics youtube.readonlyTo review the channel's own videos and plan new ones
Analytics for the channel's own videos: views, watch time, audience retention yt-analytics.readonlyTo see where viewers stop watching and improve future videos
Comments on the channel's own videos, including the commenter's public display name and comment text youtube.readonlyTo read and answer viewer questions. The app never posts replies; the owner does that on YouTube.
Uploading the owner's own videos and thumbnails youtube.uploadTo upload videos the owner produced. Uploads are always set to private; the owner publishes them manually in YouTube Studio.

The app does not access any other channel's private data. It cannot delete or edit existing videos, and it does not post comments.

4. How data is used and stored

  • On the owner's computer. Data the app fetches is shown to the owner and, where useful, saved in local files on their computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored locally in files readable only by the owner's computer account.
  • AI assistance. When the owner asks for help (for example, drafting replies or reviewing performance), the relevant data is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to produce that answer, on the owner's request only. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Not sold or shared. No data is sold, rented, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used to build profiles of viewers.
  • No cookies or tracking. The app has no website analytics, cookies or trackers. This page has none either.

5. Retention and deletion

Local files remain until the owner deletes them. Stored data that the app no longer needs is deleted, and in any case data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's developer policies require. Sign-in tokens can be deleted at any time by running the app's logout command or removing the token files.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through Google's security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer read or upload anything.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

9. Terms of use

YouTube Bridge is provided for the channel owner's personal use, as is, without warranty. Using it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.
